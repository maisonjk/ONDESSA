## The short version

Ondessa keeps your health information on your device. We do not have an account system, we do not receive your cycle, fertility, pregnancy or symptom data, and we do not sell or share it. There is no advertising or analytics software in the app. If you choose to share an update, a cycle recap or a calendar file with someone, the app hands it to an app you pick (for example Messages, email or your calendar) and you send it yourself; we never receive it, and it includes only the items you switched on.

## 1. Information that stays on your device

Everything you enter stays on your device:

- Period dates, flow, symptoms, mood, energy, sleep, pain, cervical mucus, basal body temperature, ovulation test results, intimacy entries (only if you turn them on), notes, pregnancy dates, appointments and settings.
- All predictions and insights, which are calculated on your device.

We cannot access this information. We have no copy of it and cannot recover it for you.

**Passcode encryption.** If you set a passcode, Ondessa encrypts this information on your device with AES-256, using a key derived from your passcode. If you turn on Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint unlock, your passcode is stored in your device's secure keychain on that device only. It is never synced.

**Device backups.** Your phone's own backup service may include app data in device backups that you control. On iPhone this is iCloud Backup or a computer backup; Apple encrypts these, and passcode-encrypted Ondessa data stays encrypted inside them. On Android, Ondessa opts out of automatic cloud backup.

**Backups you create.** You can export a backup file and choose where to save it. Encrypted backups require a password we never see. An unencrypted export is readable by anyone who obtains the file.

## 3. What we never do

- Sell or share personal or health information.
- Use advertising, behavioural advertising, analytics, attribution or tracking software.
- Send your health information to any artificial intelligence service.
- Put health information in notifications. Discreet wording is on by default.

## 4. Your choices and rights

- **Delete:** You → Privacy center → Delete everything permanently removes all Ondessa data from your device. Deleting the app also removes it.
- **Export:** Privacy center → Backup & export.
- **Notifications:** turn reminders on or off in Settings, or in your device's notification settings.
- **Subscriptions:** manage or cancel in your App Store or Google Play account.
