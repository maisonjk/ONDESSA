# Editorial Policy

1. **Source standard.** Only primary guidance from national health bodies (NHS, CDC, NICHD), professional societies (ACOG, ASRM, FIGO, The Menopause Society, RCOG) or peer-reviewed research. Every article lists its sources in the app, and `npm run release:check` fails if one doesn't.
2. **Drafting.** Engineers or writers may draft. AI tools may assist drafting. No content is generated at runtime.
3. **Framing.** Articles, pregnancy week notes and predictions are general information, never personal medical advice. The app does not claim that content has been reviewed or approved by a medical professional.
4. **Cadence.** Re-check sources every 12 months, or within 30 days of a guideline change.
5. **Scope.** General education only. No personalised advice, dosing or diagnosis. Articles point to a doctor or midwife for personal questions, and the pregnancy screens keep the "when to call" safety guidance.
