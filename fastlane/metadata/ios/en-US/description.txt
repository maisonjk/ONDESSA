Ondessa is a private cycle, fertility and pregnancy tracker that keeps your data on your phone. No account. No ads. No data sold. Ever.

KNOW WHAT'S COMING
Period and fertile-window predictions that show how sure they are. Every estimate comes with an honest range, and it gets sharper every cycle you log.

MY CYCLE, MY PATTERNS
Ondessa links your symptoms, mood and energy to your own cycle. Headaches before your period? Better sleep in your follicular phase? You'll see patterns from your logs, never generic averages.

TRYING TO CONCEIVE
See your best days with the honest range. Add LH tests, basal body temperature and cervical mucus if you want more detail, or skip them entirely.

PREGNANCY, WEEK BY WEEK
A calm week-by-week calendar, appointment reminders and space to note how you feel. Use the due date from your doctor or midwife or let Ondessa estimate one.

IRREGULAR OR CHANGING CYCLES
Support for perimenopause and irregular cycles that stays honest when your patterns shift.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your health data lives only on this device. Lock it with a passcode and AES-256 encryption, unlock with Face ID, create encrypted backups, export anytime, and delete everything permanently in one tap.

LOG IN SECONDS
Bleeding, symptoms, mood, energy, notes. Skip anything you don't care about.

MEMBERSHIP
Ondessa is funded by members, not advertisers. Setup is free and you see your first prediction before choosing a plan.
Ondessa Membership is available as a yearly ($59.99) or monthly ($8.99) auto-renewing subscription. Prices may vary by region and are shown in the app before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Your subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID account settings.

IMPORTANT
Ondessa provides estimates for understanding your body. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose any condition, cannot confirm ovulation or pregnancy, and is not a form of contraception. Talk to a healthcare professional about medical concerns.
